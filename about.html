OUR STORY: ACAI LIFE DA NANG

Hey there, welcome to ACAI LIFE! We popped up on Da Nang’s beachside in 2026 with a simple mission: bringing the best açaí experience in the world right down to the water’s edge. As a proud family-owned local spot, we don’t just serve bowls—we’re here to redefine what real, premium açaí is all about.

THE BRAZILIAN AÇAÍ STANDARD

Real flavor starts right at the source. We import our açaí straight from the Brazilian Amazon using 14% açaí solids—the absolute highest grade you can get. By skipping industry shortcuts, powders, and cheap fillers, every single spoonful delivers that deep, velvety richness and pure Amazonian goodness.

HOUSE-MADE CRAFTSMANSHIP

We don’t just source good stuff; we love crafting our recipes from scratch right in-house:

  • Designed for the Climate: Crafted specifically for our soft-serve machines so our açaí stays smooth and velvety—even in Vietnam’s tropical coastal heat.
  • House-Baked Granola: We bake our signature standard and dedicated gluten-free granolas fresh daily for a clean, deeply satisfying crunch.
  • Fresh-Cut Integrity: All of our fruit toppings are hand-selected daily and prepped fresh to order—pure, raw, and handled with care.
  • Artisanal Drizzles: We slow-roast and stone-grind our nut and coconut butters in-house for unmatched purity and creamy flavor.

AÇAÍ BOWLS

Whether you're recovering post-workout, grabbing a light breakfast, or treating yourself after a long beach day, our menu fits right into your rhythm:

  • Sized for Every Hunger: Available in 3 size options to suit any appetite, from a quick snack to a full meal.
  • Curated Perfection: Choose from 6 signature chef-crafted bowls for a tried-and-true favorite.
  • Unleash Creativity: Build your custom bowl from 30+ premium toppings, ranging from vibrant superfoods to house-made drizzles.
  • Tailored to You: From clean, macro-focused fuel to rich, indulgent bowls, every combo adapts to your mood.

SUPERFOOD SMOOTHIES

Handcrafted, made-to-order, and packed with functional nutrients, our superfood smoothies give you clean, refreshing energy:

  • Premium Bases: Blended exclusively on barista-grade oat milk or pure coconut water.
  • Zero Additives: Powered by our house-made nut and coconut butters—free from emulsifiers, added oils, or hidden sugars.
  • Pure Protein: Boosted with clean, hormone-free whey protein from 100% grass-fed dairy—pure, simple, and farm-direct.
  • Superfood Blends: Formulated with top-tier, nutrient-dense ingredients for clean, functional energy.

VEGAN & GLUTEN-FREE FRIENDLY

We believe everyone should enjoy clean fuel without compromise. Our menu is built for almost every diet:

  • Dietary-Friendly Base: Our core açaí soft-serve is naturally dairy-free, vegan, and gluten-free.
  • Mostly Gluten-Free & Vegan-Friendly: Almost everything on our menu is naturally vegan-friendly and gluten-free—we even bake a dedicated gluten-free granola so everyone gets that satisfying crunch.
  • Clear Labeling: Simple menu labels make finding options that fit your diet effortless.

WHOLESALE & PARTNERSHIPS

At ACAI LIFE, no detail is too small to perfect. We love pairing world-class Brazilian imports with the fresh, vibrant fruits of Vietnam. Beyond our beachfront spot, we also supply premium açaí to regional wholesale partners who share our passion for quality.
